Website Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

This policy explains what happens to information when you visit the Made by Waleed website at madeby.waleedkhan.agency (“the site”). It covers the free tools on the site. Each app has its own privacy policy, linked from its page.

Summary

The tools run in your browser. What you type, press or convert stays on your device. The site itself uses Google Analytics to measure visits, shows ads from Google and other advertising partners, and includes Amazon affiliate links.

The free tools

  • Keyboard Tester, Mouse Tester and Gamepad Tester read input from your device only while the page is open. We do not record or send what you press, click or do.
  • Universal Converter and Document Converter do their work in your browser. Numbers and files you convert are not uploaded to our servers.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how many people visit each page and how they find the site. It collects information such as the pages you view, how long you stay, your approximate location, and your browser and device type. Google Analytics sets cookies for this. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Advertising

Some pages show ads from Google AdSense and other advertising networks. These partners may use cookies or similar technologies to show ads, including ads based on your earlier visits to this site or other sites. You can manage Google’s ad personalisation at adssettings.google.com. Other partners have their own privacy policies, which you can read on their websites.

Affiliate links

Some pages link to products on Amazon. As an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases. If you click one of these links, Amazon may set its own cookies, and its privacy notice applies once you are on Amazon. We do not receive your personal details from Amazon.

Cookies

Cookies are small files a website stores in your browser. Google Analytics and the advertising partners above set cookies. Most browsers let you block or delete cookies in their settings. Blocking cookies may affect ads and analytics but will not stop the tools from working.

Children

The site is not aimed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site changes. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.
