Privacy.

Privacy policy for Istiqamah, an Android app by Made by Waleed. Last updated 22 October 2026.

What we collect.

Account details

When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture from your Google account. We use them to identify your account in the app.

Your prayer history

Your prayer logs are saved to your own Google Drive account. We do not copy them to our own servers and we cannot read them outside the app.

Usage and device information

We use Firebase Analytics to collect how the app is used (screens opened, features used) and basic device information such as device model, Android version and app version.

Ads.

Istiqamah does not show ads.

Sign-in.

Signing in with Google is handled by Google. You can remove the app's access at any time from your Google Account settings.

Sharing.

We do not sell your personal information. Data is shared only with the Google services named above (Firebase and, where stated, Google AdMob and Google Sign-In), which process it under the Google Privacy Policy.

Children.

Istiqamah is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices.

You can uninstall the app at any time. To ask what we hold about you, or to have your data deleted, email khanlabs@waleedkhan.agency.

Changes and contact.

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date above. Questions? Email khanlabs@waleedkhan.agency. Istiqamah is published by Made by Waleed, a brand of Waleed Khan Agency, Peshawar, Pakistan.
