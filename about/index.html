About.

Made by Waleed is the product side of Waleed Khan Agency. We build simple Android apps and free web tools, in Peshawar.

// what

What we make.

Small, focused apps that do one job well: tracking your prayers, saving statuses, scanning documents. Alongside them, free browser tools for testing your keyboard, mouse and controller.

// how

How we build.

Simple screens, only the permissions an app really needs, and your data kept close to you. Status Saver and Waraq Scanner keep your files on your phone, and Istiqamah keeps your prayer history on your phone too.

Our web tools run entirely in your browser. Nothing you type or click is sent anywhere.

// who

Who we are.

Made by Waleed is run by Waleed Khan and is part of Waleed Khan Agency, a strategy, design, marketing and technology agency. Our apps are published on Google Play as Made by Waleed.
