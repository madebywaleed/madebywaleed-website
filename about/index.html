About.

Made by Waleed is the product side of Waleed Khan Agency. We build simple Android apps and free web tools, in Peshawar.

// what

What we make.

We make things people can use straight away: Android apps for everyday tasks, and free tools that open in your browser with no sign-up. The same work also covers the guides, help pages and privacy policies that go with each app.

  • Android apps for trackers, scanners, file and document tools, reminders, shared task lists and vehicle logs.
  • Free browser tools such as device testers, document and unit converters, calculators, QR codes and lookup tools for names, domains and email setup.
  • Guides and help pages written in plain language, for apps and for tools.
  • Websites for businesses, through Waleed Khan Agency.

// examples

Examples we have made.

  • Istiqamah: a prayer tracker for Android that helps you build a daily habit.
  • Status Saver: save WhatsApp statuses, photos and videos to your phone.
  • Waraq Scanner: scan documents with your camera into clean PDFs you can share.
  • DeviceScan Pro: check your phone's hardware in one place, from the camera and sensors to the battery and storage.
  • Yadawana: todo lists and reminders for you and the groups you share with.
  • MyRides: a vehicle log for cars and bikes, covering fuel, services, reminders and expenses.
  • Device testers: keyboard, mouse, controller, screen, touch, speaker, microphone and camera checks.
  • Document converters: PDF tools from merge and split to PDF to Word.
  • Calculators: percentages and discounts, date and age, loan EMI and BMI.
  • Utilities: a Qibla finder, a QR code generator, a Username Checker and an Email Checker.
