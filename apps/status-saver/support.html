Status Saver Support

Last updated: October 23, 2026 · Publisher: Made by Waleed

Need help with Status Saver? Email madeby@waleedkhan.agency, or read the answers below.

Frequently asked questions

The app shows no statuses.

View them in WhatsApp first, then pull down to refresh. Check that you picked the .Statuses folder.

How do I choose the folder?

Go to Settings, then Status Folder, then Change, then Android, media, com.whatsapp, WhatsApp, Media, .Statuses. Turn on hidden files if needed.

Where are saved statuses?

In your gallery and in the Saved tab.

It says “Folder access expired.”

Choose the folder again.

What about WhatsApp Business?

Use the WA BIZ switch at the top.

Is it free?

Yes, with ads.

How do I contact you?

Email madeby@waleedkhan.agency.
