// about

About the app.

Yadawana keeps your personal and shared to-do lists in one place. Set reminders for tasks, add notes and comments, attach photos or voice notes, and assign tasks to the people in your group.

Groups can also split expenses between members, so shared costs are easy to track.

Sign in with email or Google to sync your lists across devices. Invite others to a group with a code. The app is made for Android and published by Made by Waleed. Google Play link coming soon.
