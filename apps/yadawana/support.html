Yadawana Support

Questions, problems or feedback? Email us and we'll reply as soon as we can.

madeby@waleedkhan.agency

How do I create a group and invite people?

Open the app, create a group, and share its invite code with the people you want to join. They enter the code in the app to join.

Why am I not getting reminders?

Check that notifications are allowed for Yadawana in your phone's settings, and that the task has a “needed by” time. Reminders are set on the phone of the person the task is assigned to, or of the person who created it if no one is assigned.

Who can see my tasks and comments?

Members of the groups you belong to can see the tasks, notes, comments, photos and voice notes shared in those groups. Your personal tasks are visible only to you.

Can I use the list without internet?

Yes. You can add and change tasks while offline. They sync automatically when your phone is back online.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings, then Account, and choose Delete account. If you own a group with other members, delete the group or remove the other members, or transfer ownership, first. See our Privacy Policy for what happens to your data.

Is the app free?

Yes. The app is free to use and has no ads.

Where can I get help?

Email us and tell us your phone model, your Android version, and what happened. We'll reply as soon as we can.
