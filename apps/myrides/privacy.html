Privacy Policy – MyRides

Last updated: 29 October 2026

MyRides: Service, Fuel & Costs (“the app”) is made by Made by Waleed (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the app handles and how to delete it.

Summary

The app is a vehicle log for cars and bikes. You can record your vehicles, fuel fill-ups, services, reminders and expenses. The app does not require an account. Everything you enter is stored only on your phone. We do not collect, receive or share your data, and the app has no ads or analytics.

Information you enter

The app stores what you type or choose, including vehicle details such as name, make, model, year, plate number and odometer readings, photos of your vehicles, fuel fill-ups, service records, reminders, expenses and your settings (such as units and currency).

Where your information is stored

All of this information is stored only in the app's local storage on your phone. We do not have a server that receives it, and it is not uploaded, synced or backed up by us.

Notifications

The app may ask for permission to send notifications, so it can remind you when a service is due. Reminders are scheduled on your phone. No notification content is sent to us or to any other service.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your location, contacts, files, advertising ID, analytics or crash reports. The app does not use third-party tracking, and we do not sell or share your information.

Who can see your information

Nobody but you can see the information in the app, unless you share it yourself, for example by exporting a file or sending a screenshot.

Deleting your data

All the app's data is deleted when you uninstall the app. You can also remove individual vehicles, records and expenses inside the app at any time. Because we never receive your data, we cannot delete it for you, and we do not hold a copy.

Children

The app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date. If the changes are significant, we will tell you in the app.
