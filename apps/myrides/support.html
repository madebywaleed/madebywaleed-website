MyRides Support

Questions, problems or feedback? Email us and we'll reply as soon as we can.

madeby@waleedkhan.agency

Is my data backed up?

Not yet. Your vehicles, records and expenses are stored only on your phone. If you uninstall the app or lose your phone, the data is lost. Keep a note of important details, such as service dates and mileage, until backup is available.

Why am I not getting reminders?

Check that notifications are allowed for MyRides in your phone's settings. Reminders are based on the mileage or date you set for each service, so make sure the odometer reading is up to date.

Can I track more than one vehicle?

Yes. Add each car or bike in the Garage, and record fuel, services and expenses for each one separately.

Does the app need an internet connection?

No. You can use the app fully offline. It does not need an account.

How do I delete my data?

Uninstalling the app deletes all of its data from your phone. You can also remove individual vehicles, records and expenses inside the app. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Is the app free?

Yes. The app is free to use and has no ads.

Where can I get help?

Email us and tell us your phone model, your Android version, and what happened. We'll reply as soon as we can.
