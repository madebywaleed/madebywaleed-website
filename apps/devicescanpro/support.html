DeviceScan Pro Support

Questions, problems or feedback? Email us and we'll reply as soon as we can.

madeby@waleedkhan.agency

Why does a test ask for permission?

Some tests need access to the camera, microphone or location. The app asks only when you open that test, and you can skip any test. Android requires location access to list nearby Wi-Fi and Bluetooth devices.

Does the app send my data anywhere?

No. Your test results and reports stay on your phone. The app uses the network only for features you start yourself: looking up your phone model's public name and photo on Wikipedia and Wikidata, the speed test, and the public IP check. See our Privacy Policy.

How do I delete the app's data?

Uninstalling DeviceScan Pro deletes all of its data from your phone. Reports you shared or exported stay wherever you sent them.

What does "skipped" or "failed" mean?

"Skipped" means you chose not to run that test, or your phone doesn't have that hardware. "Failed" means the test ran and the check did not pass. If a failure looks wrong, please email us with your phone model and Android version.

What to include when reporting a problem

  • Your phone model and Android version
  • The test or screen where the problem happened
  • A screenshot, if you can take one
