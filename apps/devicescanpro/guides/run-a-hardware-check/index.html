How to run a hardware check

Run the tests for your phone’s camera, sensors, battery, storage and more.

DeviceScan Pro runs tests on your phone and shows the results on screen.

What it tests

  • Camera and microphone
  • Sensors, battery and storage
  • Wi-Fi and Bluetooth
  • Vibration, NFC and the fingerprint or face sensor

Reading the results

  • Skipped: you chose not to run that test, or your phone does not have that hardware.
  • Failed: the test ran and the check did not pass.

If a failure looks wrong, email us with your phone model and Android version.

Your results

Results and reports stay on your phone. A report leaves your phone only when you share or export it yourself.
