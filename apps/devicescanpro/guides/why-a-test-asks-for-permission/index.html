Why a test asks for permission

Each permission is used only for the test that needs it, and only when you open that test.

The app asks for a permission only when you open the test that needs it. You can skip any test.

Camera

Used only in the camera test to show a live preview. Nothing is recorded or saved.

Microphone

Used only in the microphone test to show a live level. Nothing is recorded or saved.

Location and Bluetooth scan

Used only in the wireless test. Android needs location access to list nearby Wi-Fi networks, Bluetooth devices and GPS. Results are shown on screen and are not stored or sent anywhere.

Fingerprint and face

The app does not read your fingerprint or face data. It only checks whether the sensor is ready.
