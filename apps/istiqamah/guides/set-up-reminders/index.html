How to set up prayer reminders

Turn on reminders and allow the permissions Android needs so they arrive on time.

Reminders are scheduled on your phone by Android. No server is involved.

Step 1: Turn reminders on

Turn reminders on in the app and allow notifications when Android asks.

Step 2: Allow exact alarms (Android 12 and later)

Open the app’s settings in Android and allow “Alarms & reminders”. This lets reminders arrive at the exact time.

Step 3: Turn off battery restrictions

Set the app’s battery use to unrestricted. Some phones stop background alarms to save power.

Step 4: Open the app now and then

Upcoming reminders are scheduled ahead, about a month at a time. Opening the app from time to time keeps them up to date.

Still not working?

Check that notifications and alarms are allowed in Android settings, then email us with your phone model and Android version.
