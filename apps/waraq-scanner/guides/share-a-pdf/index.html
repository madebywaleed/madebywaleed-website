How to share a PDF

Share a scanned document, or save it, in the file size you need.

Share a document

  • Open the document you want to send.
  • Tap Share, then choose any app on your phone.

Save a document

You can also save the PDF to Downloads or to a folder you pick.

Choose the file size

Before you share, choose Original, Medium or Small. Smaller files are easier to send, and Original keeps the most detail.

Good to know

Once you share a PDF, the copy is handled by the app or service you chose, under its own privacy policy.
