Privacy Policy – LmarSys

Last updated: 15 November 2026

LmarSys (“the app”, “the dashboard”) is made by MadeByWaleed (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information LmarSys collects and where it goes.

Summary

LmarSys shows live readings from a solar inverter. It does not need an account. The readings come from your own inverter's Wi-Fi logger over your home network, and we do not receive them.

The demo on this website

The demo on the LmarSys page uses made-up numbers generated in your browser. It does not connect to any inverter and does not collect or send any readings. The rest of this website uses Google Analytics to measure visits and shows ads. See the website privacy policy for details.

The PC version and the planned app

The PC version available now, and the Android app planned later, are designed to work like this:

  • Your inverter data: the app talks directly to your Wi-Fi data logger on your local network. Power, battery, grid and temperature readings are shown on your device and are not sent to us.
  • Settings on your device: the logger's address and serial number, and your display choices, are stored on your own device only.
  • No account: you do not sign in, and we do not hold a copy of your readings.

Before any release, we will update this policy to list exactly what that version collects, including any ads, analytics or permissions it uses.

What we do not collect

Through the dashboard itself, we do not collect your name, email, location, contacts or inverter readings. We do not sell your information.

Children

LmarSys is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
