Privacy Policy – DeviceScan Pro

Last updated: 27 October 2026

DeviceScan Pro (“the app”) is made by Made by Waleed (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what the app does with information.

Summary

The app does not collect, sell or share your personal information. It has no account, no ads and no analytics. Everything stays on your phone, except the optional network features described below (phone-model lookup, speed test and public IP check), which you start yourself.

Information the app reads on your phone

To show device details and run hardware tests, the app reads information from your phone, such as the model, hardware specs, battery state, sensor readings and storage size. This stays on your phone.

Permissions

  • Camera: only in the camera test, to show a live preview. Nothing is recorded or saved.
  • Microphone: only in the microphone test, to show a live level. Nothing is recorded or saved.
  • Location and Bluetooth scan: only in the wireless test, because Android requires location access to list nearby Wi-Fi networks, Bluetooth devices and GPS. Results are shown on screen and are not stored or sent anywhere.
  • Internet and network state: used for the optional network features described below.
  • Vibrate, NFC, biometric: used to test those parts. The app does not read your fingerprint or face data; it only checks whether the sensor is ready.

Network use

When you ask the app to identify your phone model, it sends your phone's model code to Wikipedia and Wikidata (run by the Wikimedia Foundation) and downloads the public name and photo. No account or personal identifier is sent. Wikimedia's own privacy policy applies to those requests.

When you tap "Start test" in the speed test, the app downloads and uploads test data (up to about 100 MB) with Cloudflare's speed test servers. Cloudflare receives your IP address as part of any internet connection. When you tap "Check my IP", the app asks ipinfo.io for your public IP address and internet provider; ipinfo.io receives your IP address. These requests happen only when you tap the button. The app does not store the results beyond the screen you are viewing. Cloudflare's and ipinfo.io's own privacy policies apply to those requests.

Data stored on your phone

Test results, reports and the downloaded model name and photo are saved in the app's private storage on your phone. Reports leave your phone only when you choose to share or export them. Uninstalling the app deletes this data.

Third parties

The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking services. When you start the speed test or the public IP check, your IP address is sent to Cloudflare or ipinfo.io as described under Network use. No other data is shared with third parties.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
