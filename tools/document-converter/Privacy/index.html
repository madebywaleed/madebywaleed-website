Document Converter privacy policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Your files stay on your device

Every conversion tool runs in your browser. The files you add are read, changed and saved on your own device. They are not uploaded to our servers, and we do not receive, copy or store them.

Information we receive

We do not have accounts, and the converter does not ask for your name, email address or any other personal details. Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, may record basic technical details each time a page is requested, such as your IP address, browser type, the page you asked for and the time. This keeps the site running and secure.

Advertising

The Document Converter shows advertisements from third-party networks. Adsterra provides the advertisements in the side panels. These networks may set cookies and similar technologies, and may use information about your visits to show you advertisements they think are relevant. Google AdSense code is also loaded on these pages.

Each advertising network handles the information it collects under its own privacy policy. You can read those policies on the networks' websites.

You can limit personalised advertising through your browser settings, by clearing cookies, or with an ad blocker. Google users can change their ad settings at Google Ads Settings, and you can opt out of many networks at aboutads.info.

Services that load files for the tools

When you open a tool, your browser downloads the tool's libraries from cdnjs.cloudflare.com. The OCR tool also downloads its English language data from cdn.jsdelivr.net the first time you use it. Those requests do not include the files you convert. The page also loads fonts from Google Fonts.

Your choices

You do not need to create an account or give us information to use the converter. If you do not want advertisements, use an ad blocker or a browser that blocks them.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed. For questions about this policy, email madeby@waleedkhan.agency. For the rest of the Made by Waleed site, read the site privacy policy.
