Free Online PDF Tools: Merge, Split, Compress, Convert and Edit PDF Files

Use our free PDF tools to merge PDFs, split pages, compress files, convert PDF to Word, Excel and PowerPoint, turn images and HTML into PDF, edit and sign PDFs, and run OCR on scanned documents. Every tool works in your web browser and needs no installation. Your files are processed on your own device and are never uploaded, so your documents stay private.

#1 Merge PDF Combine PDFs in the order you want into one file. #2 Compress PDF Reduce file size. Pages are saved as images, so text can no longer be selected. #3 PDF to Word Extract the text into an editable Word file. Layout and images are not kept. #4 Split PDF Separate one page or a whole set into independent PDF files. #5 Images to PDF Combine PNG, JPG, WEBP or AVIF images into one PDF. #6 PDF to Images Save each PDF page as a PNG, or as a ZIP for longer files. #7 Edit PDF Add text to a page at a position you choose. Existing text is not changed. #8 Sign PDF Add a typed signature to a page of your PDF. #9 PDF to Excel Pull text and simple tables from a PDF into an Excel spreadsheet. #10 PDF to PowerPoint Turn each PDF page into a slide. Pages are images, so the text can't be edited. #11 CSV to Excel Turn CSV or TSV files into Excel (.xlsx) spreadsheets. #12 OCR PDF Read the text in scanned pages so it can be searched and selected. English only, and slow. #13 Rotate PDF Turn pages 90, 180 or 270 degrees, all pages or chosen ones. #14 Watermark Stamp faint text, such as DRAFT or CONFIDENTIAL, across every page. #15 Redact PDF Black out chosen words. The pages are flattened, so the hidden text is removed too. #16 Organize PDF Reorder pages, or remove the ones you don't need. #17 Page numbers Add page numbers to every page, with your choice of position. #18 Crop PDF Trim the same margin from every edge of every page. #19 Scan to PDF Take photos of documents with your phone, or add images, and combine them into one PDF. #20 PDF to Markdown Turn the text of a PDF into a Markdown (.md) file, with headings kept where they're clear. #21 HTML to PDF Paste HTML and save it as a PDF. Scripts are removed. #22 PDF Forms Add fillable text fields and checkboxes to a page. #23 Compare PDF Highlight what changed between an old and a new version of a PDF. #24 Repair PDF Try to fix a damaged PDF by rebuilding its structure.

About these tools

Each conversion has its own page. Choose one above to open it. Every tool runs in your browser: your files are read, changed and saved on your own device, and nothing is uploaded.

Very large files can be slow on older devices, because the work uses your device's memory. For the privacy details, read the Document Converter privacy policy.
