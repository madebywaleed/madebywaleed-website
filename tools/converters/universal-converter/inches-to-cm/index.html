How to convert inches to centimetres

The inch is used for everyday lengths in the US and the UK, such as screen sizes and heights. One inch is exactly 2.54 centimetres.

To convert inches to centimetres, multiply the inches by 2.54. To go the other way, divide the centimetres by 2.54.

Example: 12 in = 30.48 cm.
