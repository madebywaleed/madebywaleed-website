How to convert seer to kg

The seer is a traditional unit of weight used in Pakistan and parts of South Asia for grain, sugar, flour and other goods. One seer is about 0.9331 kilograms, or roughly 933 grams. There are 40 seers in a maund.

To convert seer to kg, multiply the number of seers by 0.9331. To go the other way, divide the kilograms by 0.9331.

Example: 5 seer = 4.6655 kg.

Common questions

How many kg is 1 seer?

1 seer is about 0.9331 kilograms, or roughly 933 grams.

How many seers are in 1 kg?

1 kilogram is about 1.0717 seers.

How many seers make a maund?

There are 40 seers in 1 maund, which is about 37.32 kilograms.

Is the seer the same everywhere?

This page uses the standard South Asian seer of about 933 grams. Some regions and trades used slightly different seers in the past, so check the local definition if exact weight matters.
