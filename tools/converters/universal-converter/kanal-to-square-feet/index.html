How to convert kanal to square feet

The kanal is a unit of land area used in Pakistan and parts of India. In Pakistan 1 kanal is 5,445 square feet (about 505.86 square metres), which is 20 marla. Sizes can vary by region, so check the local definition before you buy or sell land.

To convert kanal to square feet, multiply the number of kanal by 5,445. To go the other way, divide the square feet by 5,445.

Example: 2 kanal = 10,890 sq ft.

Common questions

How many square feet are in 1 kanal?

1 kanal is 5,445 square feet.

How many acres is 1 kanal?

1 acre is 43,560 square feet, so 1 kanal is about 0.125 acres (one eighth of an acre).

Is a kanal the same everywhere?

No. Sizes vary by region, so confirm the definition in your property documents.
