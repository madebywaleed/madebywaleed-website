How to use the Universal AI Converter

The Universal AI Converter turns a plain-language request into a clear answer. You do not need to choose a unit type or learn a formula. Write what you want converted, the way you would say it to a friend, and the converter sends it to Google’s Gemini model, which returns the result.

  1. Paste your Gemini API key. You can create a free key in Google AI Studio.
  2. Type your conversion, for example “100 USD to AED” or “5 cups to ml”.
  3. Press Convert Now. The answer appears in the panel, with a short note on how it was worked out.

Keep requests short and specific. Naming both units, and the thing being measured, gives the most reliable answer. For example, “1 cup of flour to grams” is clearer than “cup to grams”.

Supported conversion types

You can ask for almost any everyday conversion. The converter handles four main groups well:

Currencies

Convert between major currencies, such as USD, EUR, GBP, AED, PKR and SAR. Exchange rates change every day, so the result is an estimate. Check the live rate with your bank before you send money.

Metric and imperial units

Length, weight, volume, temperature, speed and area. Examples include kilometres to miles, kilograms to pounds, and Celsius to Fahrenheit.

Cooking measures

Cups, spoons and ounces to millilitres or grams, and oven temperatures between Celsius, Fahrenheit and gas marks. Ingredient weights vary, so grams per cup depends on the food. Say the ingredient for a better answer.

Custom AI conversions

Anything else you can describe, such as converting a shoe size between countries, a paper size, or time zones. The answer is generated by AI, so for safety, medical or legal decisions, confirm with an official source.

Tips for accurate answers

  • Name the currency code or unit clearly, such as USD rather than dollars.
  • Add the ingredient or item for cooking and weight conversions.
  • Ask one conversion at a time to keep the answer focused.
  • Check important figures before you rely on them.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Universal AI Converter free?

Yes. The tool is free to use. Google may charge for heavy use of the Gemini API, depending on your plan. Check Google AI Studio for current limits and prices.

Where do I get a Gemini API key?

Sign in with your Google account in Google AI Studio, then create an API key. Paste it into the key field above.

Are the currency rates live?

No. The converter asks an AI model for an estimate, and the model may not have today’s rate. Use it for a rough idea, and check your bank or an official source for exact figures.

Why did I get an error?

The most common reasons are a missing or mistyped key, a key that Google has not enabled, or reaching your free usage limit. The message in the result panel says which one it is. Wait a minute if you hit a limit, then try again.

Does the converter store my key or my requests?

No. We do not store the key or your requests on our servers. Your browser sends the request directly to Google.

Can it convert things that are not units?

Sometimes. It works well for clear everyday conversions. For something unusual, describe it in detail and check the answer.
