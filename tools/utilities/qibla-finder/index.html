Computation method
    Note: Times are worked out in your browser and shown in your device's time zone. They are approximate, within about two minutes. Check them against your local mosque's timetable.

    // about/

    About this tool.

    The Qibla is the direction of the Kaaba in Makkah. This tool works out the great-circle bearing from your position, and shows the bearing and distance to Masjid Al-Aqsa and the angle between the two. The sun's position and prayer times are worked out from your position and the date.

    Your location is read by your browser only when you press the button. The page saves it in your browser on this device, so it can find you again next time. It is never uploaded or shared. The tool does not use a map service.

    // faq/

    Questions.

    Why does it ask for my location?

    To work out the bearings and prayer times from where you are. Without your location, no direction can be calculated. If you prefer not to share it, type your latitude and longitude instead.

    Is the direction exact?

    The calculation is exact for your position. The compass depends on your phone's sensors, so it can be a few degrees off. Use a dedicated compass for the final check, and ask a knowledgeable person if you need a ruling for a particular place.

    Does it work on iPhone?

    Yes. On iPhone, the compass needs your permission, which you give when you press "Turn on compass". Without it, the bearings, distances and prayer times still work.

    // privacy/

    Your privacy

    Your location is saved only in your browser on this device, so the page can remember it. We do not store it or send it to our servers. Compass readings are used live and are not saved. To delete the saved location, clear this site's data in your browser settings. Read the website privacy policy for details.
