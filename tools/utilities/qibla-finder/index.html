Manual position

Or type your position. Use decimals, for example 34.00 and 71.58 for Peshawar.

Without the compass, the number is the direction from true north. Turn yourself until the arrow on the dial points to the top of the screen, or use the number with a compass app.

Phone compasses can be off near metal or magnets. Move the phone in a figure of eight to calibrate it. The dial shows "Aligned" when the phone is within 3° of the Qibla.

    // about/

    About this tool.

    The Qibla is the direction of the Kaaba in Makkah. This tool works out the direction from your position using the great-circle bearing, and shows the distance to the Kaaba.

    Your location is read by your browser only when you press the button, and it is used only to calculate the direction. It is not stored, uploaded or shared. The tool does not use a map service.

    // faq/

    Questions.

    Why does it ask for my location?

    To work out the Qibla from where you are. Without your location, no direction can be calculated. If you prefer not to share it, type your latitude and longitude instead.

    Is the direction exact?

    The calculation is exact for your position. The compass depends on your phone's sensors, so it can be a few degrees off. Use a dedicated compass for the final check, and ask a knowledgeable person if you need a ruling for a particular place.

    Does it work on iPhone?

    Yes. On iPhone, the compass needs your permission, which you give when you press "Turn on compass". Without it, the direction and distance still work.

    Your privacy

    Your location and compass reading stay on your device. We do not store them or send them to our servers. Read the website privacy policy for details.
