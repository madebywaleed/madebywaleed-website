// about/

Free tools for everyone.

Made by Waleed builds small, free tools that run in your web browser. You do not need to download anything, create an account, or pay. Each tool is built to do one job well, so you can get an answer in seconds and go back to what you were doing.

Every tool on this page is built the same way. Open it, use it, and close it. The four tools cover different everyday jobs, so pick the one that matches what you need.

  • Keyboard Tester checks every key, rollover and double-press, and estimates polling rate and input delay. Useful when a key feels sticky or a gaming keyboard seems slow.
  • Mouse Tester checks every button and the scroll wheel, catches double-clicks, and estimates polling rate and DPI. Useful for spotting a worn switch before it fails.
  • Gamepad Tester checks every button, trigger and stick on Xbox, PlayStation and Nintendo controllers. Useful for finding stick drift and checking vibration.
  • Universal ConverterDocument Converter handles metric and imperial units, cooking measures, temperature, speed, area, time, data, pressure, energy, fuel economy and South Asian units, along with currencies. Choose a type, pick the two units, and type the amount. For currency, enter today’s rate from your bank or a trusted source, because the converter does not supply live rates.

Your privacy

These tools run in your browser. Nothing you type, click or convert is sent to our servers, and we do not ask for your name, email or any account details to use them. Read the privacy policies for the details of what each app and site stores.

// faq/

Frequently asked questions.

Are these tools free?

Yes. Every tool on this page is free to use, and you do not need to create an account.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Open the tool in a modern web browser such as Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari, and it is ready to use.

Are my results private?

The tools run on your device. We do not send what you type, click or convert to our servers, so there is no record of your results on our side.

How accurate is the Universal Converter?

Standard unit conversions use fixed conversion factors, so they are exact to the precision shown. Currency results are only as accurate as the rate you enter, so check it against a current source before making a payment or a decision.
