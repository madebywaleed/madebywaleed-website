Device testers.

Test your keyboard, mouse, game controller, screen, touchscreen, speakers, microphone and camera right in the browser. Free, no sign-up, and nothing you do leaves your computer.

What is a device tester?

A device tester is a web page that checks how the hardware on your computer or phone responds. Keyboard, mouse and gamepad testers show each key, button and stick as you press it. The screen, touchscreen, speaker, microphone and webcam testers check your display, touch input, sound output, microphone input and camera. They help you find faults such as dead keys, double-clicking mice, stick drift, dead pixels, ghost touches, silent speakers and faulty cameras, before you rely on the device.

Questions

Are the device testers free?

Yes. All eight testers are free, need no account, and you can use them as often as you like.

Do the tests send my data anywhere?

The tests run in your browser. The microphone and camera are used only while a test is running, and nothing you do is sent to our servers.

Why does the gamepad tester need a button press?

Browsers only detect a controller after you press a button on it. Connect the controller, press any button, and the tester picks it up.

Which browser works best?

A current version of a modern browser works best. Some features, such as controller detection, camera resolution and microphone device names, depend on what the browser reports.

About device testers.

These tests help you check whether a keyboard, mouse or game controller is working as it should. They run in your browser, so there is nothing to install. Each tester shows what it measured on the screen, and nothing is sent to our servers.

Are these testers free?

Yes. There is no sign-up, and you can use each tester as often as you like.

How accurate is the polling rate?

The testers estimate polling rate from the timing of each input. Browsers limit timing precision, so treat the figure as a guide rather than an exact measurement.

Why does the gamepad tester need a button press?

Browsers only detect a controller after you press a button on it. Connect the controller, press any button, and the tester will pick it up.

Your privacy

These tools run in your browser. Nothing you type, click or convert is sent to our servers, and we do not ask for your name, email or any account details to use them. Read the privacy policies for the details of what each app and site stores.
